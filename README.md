# **Kerbal Generations**

**Kerbal Generations** is a lightweight population and dynasty mod for Kerbal Space Program. It allows your Kerbals to have children, grow up, and form complex family trees, making it the perfect companion for long-term colonization saves.

## **Features**

* **Natural Population Growth:** If you have a Male and Female Kerbal on the same vessel with enough empty seats, nature will take its course.  
* **The Nursery System:** Babies are born as **Tourists**. They can travel but cannot pilot or work.  
* **Dynamic Maturation:** After a set childhood duration (Default: 1 Kerbin Year), babies mature into a random profession (Pilot, Engineer, or Scientist) and join your workforce.  
* **Family Tree Tracking:** The mod logs the Father and Mother of every child born.  
* **Renaming Utility:** A built-in renamer (press 'R' in the UI) allows you to rename Kerbals without breaking their family links.  
* **Safety First:** Breeding automatically pauses if the ship is too full or lacks resources.

## **Installation**

1. Download the .zip file.  
2. Open the zip and find the GameData folder.  
3. Drag the KerbalGenerations folder into your KSP installation's GameData folder.  
   * *Correct path:* Kerbal Space Program/GameData/KerbalGenerations/Plugins/KerbalGenerations.dll

## **How to Use**

1\. The Interface  
Once in flight (controlling a ship), press F7 to open the Kerbal Generations control window.  
**2\. Breeding Mechanics**

* **Requirements:** At least 1 Adult Male and 1 Adult Female on the active vessel, plus at least 2 Empty Seats.  
* **Pregnancy:** Progress happens automatically. You can adjust the **Breeding Speed** slider to speed up gestation (useful for testing) or slow it down for realism.

**3\. Growing Up**

* Babies spawn as Tourists.  
* Use the **Childhood Duration** slider to decide how long they stay children (Default: \~426 Days).  
* Once the time passes, you will receive a notification that they have grown up and been assigned a class.
